# Anti-Patterns and Design Critique Reference

One of Pixasso's primary responsibilities is to detect, call out, and eliminate generic AI-generated design clichés. When a proposed design looks generic, Pixasso explicitly states: "This looks generic," diagnoses the specific root cause, and provides a distinct, intentional alternative.

---

## 1. The Impeccable AI Slop Catalog (65+ Anti-Patterns)

A comprehensive taxonomy of modern interface tropes, aesthetic clichés, and cognitive anti-patterns commonly generated by AI coding tools and low-effort templates.

### Category A: Chromatic and Atmospheric Slop

1. **Purple-to-Cyan Gradient Halo Syndrome**:
   - *Symptom*: Applying radial or linear background gradients spanning indigo, purple, and neon cyan across dark mode as the default aesthetic.
   - *Fix*: Ground the palette in authentic materials: warm ink on paper, tactile dark slate with a single sharp amber accent, olive and bone tones, or high-contrast monochromes.

2. **The Radial Background Spotlight**:
   - *Symptom*: Centering a giant blurry radial gradient blob directly behind the hero headline.
   - *Fix*: Use crisp, deliberate surface boundaries, subtle noise texture, or intentional typography without atmospheric haze.

3. **Muddy Mid-Tone Dark Mode**:
   - *Symptom*: Setting secondary text to `#4A5568` over `#121212` backgrounds, yielding contrast ratios below 2.5:1.
   - *Fix*: Calibrate text colors to maintain at least 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast (e.g., `#A1A1AA` over `#18181B`).

4. **Default Glassmorphism Everything**:
   - *Symptom*: Turning every card, modal, and navigation bar into a translucent blurred rectangle (`backdrop-filter: blur()`) with 1px semi-white borders.
   - *Fix*: Use solid, opaque panels with clear elevation, crisp borders, and deliberate contrast separation.

5. **Universal Glowing Borders**:
   - *Symptom*: Applying neon glowing box shadows or rotating animated conic gradient borders to every card.
   - *Fix*: Reserve accent borders strictly for active selections, focused inputs, or single primary callouts.

6. **Gradient Text Mask Overuse**:
   - *Symptom*: Wrapping primary headings in multi-color gradients with `background-clip: text`.
   - *Fix*: Rely on authoritative display typography in solid color with calibrated tracking.

7. **Chromatic Halation (Pure Blue on Pure Black)**:
   - *Symptom*: Placing saturated `#0000FF` text or buttons over pure `#000000` backgrounds, causing human eye lens refraction and visual vibration.
   - *Fix*: Desaturate blue to cornflower or periwinkle (`#60A5FA` or `#818CF8`) or elevate background to `#0F172A`.

8. **Halogen Neon Overdose**:
   - *Symptom*: Using 4 or 5 competing fluorescent accent colors simultaneously (neon green, electric pink, cyan, lime).
   - *Fix*: Enforce the 60-30-10 rule: 60 percent dominant ground, 30 percent structural surfaces, 10 percent single accent.

9. **Inverted Contrast Vibrancy**:
   - *Symptom*: Setting body text to 100 percent pure `#FFFFFF` on 100 percent pure `#000000`, causing eye strain over long reading sessions.
   - *Fix*: Soften body copy to zinc-200 (`#E4E4E7`) or slate-200 (`#E2E8F0`) for comfortable optical reading.

10. **The Washed-Out Pastel Soup**:
    - *Symptom*: Light mode interfaces with faint gray cards, faint gray borders, and faint gray text with zero optical focal point.
    - *Fix*: Introduce punchy dark charcoal text (`#09090B`) and crisp defined keylines.

### Category B: Layout and Spacing Slop

11. **Cardocalypse (Cards Inside Cards Inside Cards)**:
    - *Symptom*: Nesting bordered cards within larger bordered cards, creating redundant visual containers.
    - *Fix*: Eliminate container nesting. Use whitespace and typography to define hierarchy rather than endless borders.

12. **The "Three Identical Icon Cards" Section**:
    - *Symptom*: Splitting features into three identical 33 percent width cards with a pastel icon circle, a 3-word title, and a two-sentence paragraph.
    - *Fix*: Use an asymmetric bento layout where the primary differentiator commands 60 to 70 percent of grid width.

13. **Floating Badge Above Every Headline**:
    - *Symptom*: Placing a rounded pill badge with a sparkle emoji or "New Feature v2.0" directly above every single section title.
    - *Fix*: Contextualize product status in the navigation bar or within the primary copy itself.

14. **Arbitrary Spacing Inconsistency**:
    - *Symptom*: Mixing 13px, 19px, 27px, and 35px margins throughout the same component.
    - *Fix*: Lock all spacing to a strict 4px/8px geometric scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px).

15. **Giant Empty Hero Chasm**:
    - *Symptom*: Leaving a 200px blank void between the hero CTA and the below-the-fold content.
    - *Fix*: Anchor the hero with immediate proof: live product UI snapshot, interactive preview, or customer logo bar.

16. **Orphaned Grid Tiles**:
    - *Symptom*: Creating a 3-column grid with 4 or 5 items, leaving awkward trailing empty slots.
    - *Fix*: Span cards across 2 columns or adjust grid layout dynamically to ensure complete balanced rows.

17. **Crowded Container Bounds**:
    - *Symptom*: Card text running flush against borders with only 8px internal padding.
    - *Fix*: Give content room to breathe with at least 20px to 28px internal card padding.

18. **Full-Bleed Fatigue**:
    - *Symptom*: Every section spanning 100 percent edge-to-edge width without a disciplined max-width container constraint.
    - *Fix*: Restrain content within structured container widths (`max-w-6xl` or `max-w-7xl`, centered with `mx-auto`).

19. **The Unanchored Centered Button**:
    - *Symptom*: A single floating centered button in a vast white ocean without supporting context.
    - *Fix*: Group actions into clear hierarchy: primary CTA paired with secondary documentation or demo link.

20. **Asymmetrical Balance Failure**:
    - *Symptom*: Cramming heavy dense content on one side of a 2-column layout while the other side sits completely empty.
    - *Fix*: Counterbalance content mass through optical scaling, interactive widgets, or media previews.

### Category C: Typographic Slop

21. **Inter Everywhere Default**:
    - *Symptom*: Defaulting to standard system Inter for every single project regardless of brand personality, industry, or mood.
    - *Fix*: Choose deliberate display typefaces matching domain feel (e.g., editorial serifs, technical grotesques, geometric sans).

22. **Crushed Letter-Spacing on Body Copy**:
    - *Symptom*: Applying negative tracking (`tracking-tight` or `-0.05em`) to 14px or 16px body copy, causing character collisions.
    - *Fix*: Negative tracking belongs only on large display headings (32px+). Body text requires neutral or slight positive tracking.

23. **All-Caps Micro-Label Overdose**:
    - *Symptom*: Setting every subtitle, tag, button, and table header in tiny 10px uppercase bold text with wide tracking.
    - *Fix*: Reserve all-caps strictly for short acronyms or category identifiers. Rely on sentence case for readability.

24. **Inconsistent Line Heights (Leading Jumps)**:
    - *Symptom*: Mixing `leading-none`, `leading-loose`, and default line-heights arbitrarily across sibling text elements.
    - *Fix*: Standardize leading: 1.1 for display titles, 1.25 for subtitles, 1.5 to 1.6 for body copy.

25. **Centered Body Copy Blocks**:
    - *Symptom*: Center-aligning multi-sentence paragraphs longer than 3 lines.
    - *Fix*: Center alignment is only acceptable for 1 to 2 line headlines. All continuous reading blocks must be left-aligned.

26. **Orphaned Words at Paragraph Ends**:
    - *Symptom*: A paragraph ending with a single solitary 3-letter word on its final line.
    - *Fix*: Apply `text-wrap: balance` or `text-wrap: pretty` in modern CSS to balance line breaks automatically.

27. **Excessive Font Weight Variety**:
    - *Symptom*: Using 100, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, and 900 weights in a single page.
    - *Fix*: Restrict the palette to two or three calibrated weights (e.g., 400 regular, 500 medium, 600 semibold).

28. **Runaway Heading Scales**:
    - *Symptom*: H1, H2, and H3 having almost identical font sizes (24px, 22px, 20px).
    - *Fix*: Use a modular type scale (1.25 Major Third or 1.333 Perfect Fourth) for clear hierarchical steps.

29. **Unreadable Monospace Paragraphs**:
    - *Symptom*: Rendering long narrative explanatory text in Courier or JetBrains Mono.
    - *Fix*: Monospace is strictly for code, keyboard shortcuts, timestamps, and numeric data tables.

30. **Missing Hierarchy Between Title and Subtitle**:
    - *Symptom*: Title and subtitle having the exact same color and weight, differing only by 2px in size.
    - *Fix*: Differentiate with scale, weight, and secondary muted color tones.

### Category D: Component and Icon Slop

31. **Rounded Square Icon Tile Above Every Heading**:
    - *Symptom*: Stacking a 48x48px pastel rounded square with an outline icon directly above every title in a grid.
    - *Fix*: Integrate iconography into typography or replace with concrete UI component demonstrations.

32. **Lucide Icon Spam**:
    - *Symptom*: Placing decorative icons next to every word, menu item, and button label without functional utility.
    - *Fix*: Remove decorative icons. If an icon does not aid wayfinding or recognition, eliminate it.

33. **Alert Side-Tab Border Misuse**:
    - *Symptom*: Putting a 4px colored left border on ordinary feature cards (styling them as error alerts).
    - *Fix*: Left accent borders belong strictly on system notification banners and alert callouts.

34. **Sparkle Emoji Cliché**:
    - *Symptom*: Using the sparkle emoji on every AI button, heading badge, and input adornment.
    - *Fix*: Use functional, domain-specific terminology without decorative emoji crutches.

35. **Identical Toggle Switch Default**:
    - *Symptom*: Slapping a shadcn toggle switch into every settings row even when radio groups or checkboxes fit better.
    - *Fix*: Use toggles for instantaneous system state changes; use checkboxes for form submission selections.

36. **Checkbox Overload in Filters**:
    - *Symptom*: Stacking 30 unchecked checkboxes in a vertical column without search or categorization.
    - *Fix*: Use searchable multi-select comboboxes or horizontal filter chips with count indicators.

37. **Redundant Card Wrapper for Plain Text**:
    - *Symptom*: Enclosing a single sentence of plain text in a heavy bordered card with drop shadow.
    - *Fix*: Render plain text directly on the ground plane without unnecessary structural packaging.

38. **Unlabeled Icon-Only Buttons**:
    - *Symptom*: Primary action buttons represented solely by ambiguous icons with no text label or tooltip.
    - *Fix*: Always include visible text labels or clear `aria-label` attributes with instant hover tooltips.

39. **Inconsistent Border Radii**:
    - *Symptom*: Mixing sharp 0px corners, 4px rounded buttons, 16px cards, and 32px search bars randomly.
    - *Fix*: Establish a unified radius token scale (e.g., base radius 8px for controls, 16px for cards).

40. **The Universal Avatar Pile**:
    - *Symptom*: Displaying 5 overlapping circular avatars with "+2,000 satisfied users" on every hero.
    - *Fix*: Provide concrete, verifiable social proof: company logos, customer metrics, or direct case studies.

### Category E: Interaction and Motion Slop

41. **Frantic Magnetic Buttons**:
    - *Symptom*: Buttons that violently jerk toward the mouse cursor from 100px away.
    - *Fix*: Subtle hover transitions within the element's actual bounding box (`scale: 1.02` max).

42. **Sluggish Universal Scroll-Fade**:
    - *Symptom*: Animating every single section with an 800ms fade-in, forcing users to wait while scrolling.
    - *Fix*: Render content statically above and near the fold. Keep scroll animations fast (under 250ms).

43. **Cursor-Following Glow Blobs**:
    - *Symptom*: Attaching a glowing gradient circle that trails the mouse cursor everywhere.
    - *Fix*: Remove cursor trails. Use localized hover state lighting on cards only where interactive.

44. **Modal Interruption Abuse**:
    - *Symptom*: Popping up a newsletter modal, cookie banner, and discount drawer simultaneously on page load.
    - *Fix*: Keep initial page load clean. Surface dialogs only in response to explicit user triggers.

45. **Accordion Hiding Critical Information**:
    - *Symptom*: Hiding key pricing terms, core features, or essential specifications inside collapsed accordions.
    - *Fix*: Display essential information openly. Reserve accordions for secondary FAQ details.

46. **Parallax Nausea**:
    - *Symptom*: Background, midground, and text layers scrolling at wildly mismatched speeds.
    - *Fix*: Keep motion subtle and grounded. Motion should inform spatial depth, not cause disorientation.

47. **Jarring Page Transitions**:
    - *Symptom*: Full screen wipe animations that flash white or freeze navigation between route changes.
    - *Fix*: Instantaneous route updates with optimistic data loading and localized skeleton states.

48. **Infinite Scroll Without Footer Access**:
    - *Symptom*: Automatically loading more items as the user scrolls, making it impossible to reach the footer links.
    - *Fix*: Use explicit "Load More" buttons or pagination when a footer contains important links.

49. **Sticky Header Covering Content**:
    - *Symptom*: Fixed headers with excessive height that obscure anchor links when navigating via hash tags.
    - *Fix*: Apply `scroll-margin-top` to headings to ensure targets are not hidden behind sticky navigation.

50. **Missing Reduced-Motion Support**:
    - *Symptom*: Intense animations running continuously without honoring user accessibility preferences.
    - *Fix*: Wrap all keyframe animations in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to disable motion.

### Category F: Copywriting and Cognitive Slop

51. **"Supercharge Your Workflow With AI" Headline**:
    - *Symptom*: Vague, buzzword-heavy headlines that say nothing about what the product actually does.
    - *Fix*: State the concrete problem and solution directly: "Real-time database sync for distributed teams".

52. **Redundant Helper Microcopy**:
    - *Symptom*: Placing "Click the button below to submit your form" directly above a button labeled "Submit".
    - *Fix*: Delete redundant instructions. Let clear button labels communicate the action.

53. **Meaningless Metric Inflation**:
    - *Symptom*: Stating "99.9% customer happiness" or "10x productivity increase" without verifiable context.
    - *Fix*: Quote verifiable, concrete measurements: "Reduced build times from 12m to 42s".

54. **Fake Testimonial Carousels**:
    - *Symptom*: Generic stock photos with fictional names saying "This product changed my life".
    - *Fix*: Use real customer names, verified titles, company badges, and specific quotes.

55. **False Urgency Banners**:
    - *Symptom*: Fake countdown timers claiming "Offer expires in 14 minutes" that reset on refresh.
    - *Fix*: Eliminate manipulative dark patterns. Build trust through transparent pricing.

56. **Uninformative Error Messages**:
    - *Symptom*: Displaying "An unexpected error occurred" with zero recovery instructions.
    - *Fix*: Explain what went wrong and provide a recovery action: "Network timeout. Check connection and retry."

57. **Cognitive Overload Forms**:
    - *Symptom*: Demanding 18 form fields on initial sign-up before delivering any product value.
    - *Fix*: Progressive profiling: ask for email only first, collect additional preferences later.

58. **Generic CTA Buttons ("Get Started")**:
    - *Symptom*: Using "Get Started" for every button across the entire site.
    - *Fix*: Use specific action verbs: "Start Free Trial", "Create New Workspace", "Browse Catalog".

59. **Obscured Pricing Terms**:
    - *Symptom*: Advertising "$19/month" while burying annual billing commitments in tiny 8px gray text.
    - *Fix*: Make pricing transparent with an obvious monthly vs annual billing toggle.

60. **Missing Zero-State Guidance**:
    - *Symptom*: Empty dashboards showing blank grey boxes with no onboarding guidance.
    - *Fix*: Provide friendly empty states with a single clear button to create the first item.

61. **Dead-End 404 Pages**:
    - *Symptom*: Blank 404 pages with no search bar or return navigation links.
    - *Fix*: Provide search, category links, and quick recovery buttons.

62. **Tooltip Traps on Mobile**:
    - *Symptom*: Hiding critical information inside hover-only tooltips that touchscreens cannot trigger.
    - *Fix*: Show critical information inline; use tap-to-expand disclosures on mobile.

63. **Tab Overload**:
    - *Symptom*: Cramming 12 horizontal tabs into a single card header, causing horizontal overflow.
    - *Fix*: Group related tabs into categories or use a vertical secondary navigation rail.

64. **Unformatted Raw Data Tables**:
    - *Symptom*: Displaying raw ISO timestamps (`2026-10-13T17:15:21Z`) and raw integer cents in UI tables.
    - *Fix*: Format data humanely: "Oct 13, 2026" and "$12.50".

65. **Cookie Banner Wall**:
    - *Symptom*: Giant modal blocking the entire screen before any page content can be seen.
    - *Fix*: Use a compact bottom banner that preserves full access to page reading.

## 2. Pixasso Design Critique Framework

When evaluating an existing design, prototype, or code implementation, conduct an uncompromising review across five core pillars:

### Pillar 1: UX and Information Architecture
- **Clarity**: Is the core value proposition and purpose immediately obvious within 3 seconds?
- **Hierarchy**: Does the eye move naturally from primary focal point to secondary actions?
- **Cognitive Load**: Is the interface asking the user to parse too many competing signals at once?
- **Task Flow**: Can the user complete their primary objective with minimal friction and zero ambiguity?

### Pillar 2: Visual Design and Craft
- **Composition**: Is the layout balanced, whether symmetrical or dynamically asymmetrical?
- **Typography**: Are font weights, leading, and tracking harmonious? Does the display face match the brand personality?
- **Spacing System**: Are margins and padding governed by a consistent spacing token scale (4/8/16/24/32/48/64px)?
- **Color Discipline**: Is color used functionally to direct action, or applied randomly as decoration?

### Pillar 3: Interaction and Affordance
- **Discoverability**: Can users easily distinguish between interactive controls and static display containers?
- **State Completeness**: Are hover, active, focus, disabled, loading, and error states fully designed and visually distinct?
- **Touch Ergonomics**: Are tap targets on mobile viewports comfortably sized (minimum 44x44px)?

### Pillar 4: Motion and Choreography
- **Functional Value**: Does motion clarify layout changes, or does it merely delay interaction?
- **Pacing**: Are transitions crisp and snappy (under 300ms) or painfully sluggish?
- **Accessibility**: Is the entire motion suite neutralized when `prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled?

### Pillar 5: Technical and Performance Feasibility
- **DOM and Render Cost**: Are layout-triggering CSS properties being animated, or are transitions restricted to `transform` and `opacity`?
- **GPU Overhead**: Are WebGL scenes, backdrop filters, or particle systems throttled appropriately for mobile hardware?
- **Semantic Integrity**: Does the markup use correct semantic HTML elements, or is it a soup of nested unsemantic divs?

## 3. Constructive Critique Delivery Format

When providing critique, adhere to this direct, structured feedback format:

1. **Executive Verdict**: A 1-sentence verdict highlighting the central strength and central flaw.
2. **The Weakest Link**: Identify the single element that most severely degrades the experience.
3. **Anti-Pattern Diagnosis**: Explicitly name any clichés present and explain why they weaken credibility.
4. **Actionable Remediation**: Provide concrete typographic scales, token values, layout adjustments, or code diffs to elevate the interface.
