# Inspiration Galleries and Deconstruction Reference

Inspiration galleries serve as broad observational environments for studying contemporary digital craft. Pixasso approaches galleries not as a catalog of templates to clone, but as empirical case studies of visual tension, rhythm, layout systems, and interaction semantics.

## 6. Deconstruction Framework: The 8-Point Lens

Whenever inspecting a reference website or interface screenshot, decompose it across eight formal dimensions:

1. **Information Architecture and Density**: How many focal points compete for user attention in the viewport? Is information organized linearly, hierarchically, or through modular bento structures?
2. **Typography Architecture**: What is the structural relationship between display and body typefaces? What are the exact proportions (scale ratio, tracking values, line height multipliers)?
3. **Layout and Spatial Rhythm**: What grid is being used (12-column, 16-column, golden ratio, asymmetric split)? What is the baseline spacing token (4px, 8px, 12px)?
4. **Surface and Texture Treatment**: Is the surface flat, tactile, metallic, glass, or paper-inspired? How are borders and elevation treated?
5. **Interaction Feedback and Affordances**: How does the user discover that an element is interactive? What visual changes occur on hover, focus, active press, and disabled states?
6. **Motion and Choreography**: What triggers movement (time, scroll position, cursor proximity, state change)? What is the duration, curve, and sequencing offset?
7. **Responsive Transformation**: How does the layout recompose from 1440px desktop down to 390px mobile? Does navigation collapse into a drawer or segmented tab bar?
8. **Anti-Pattern Audit**: Does the design rely on generic clichés? What makes this site memorable compared to fifty similar sites in the same category?

---

## 7. Synthesis Over Imitation

Never copy an entire layout or produce a collage of recognizable websites. Deconstruct underlying principles, adjust for user context, and assemble a novel visual language tailored directly to user intent.
